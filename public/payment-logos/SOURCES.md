# Payment marks

These marks identify payment preferences in a booking interface. They do not indicate a connected gateway or a completed charge. Trademarks belong to their respective owners. Keep original artwork and proportions.

- Apple Pay: [Apple Pay wordmark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_Pay_logo.svg); [Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/).
- Google Pay: [Google Pay wordmark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Pay_Logo.svg); [Google guidelines](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines).
- Visa: [Visa 2021 mark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Visa_2021.svg).
- Mastercard: [Mastercard 2019 symbol](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mastercard_2019_logo.svg).
- American Express: [2018 mark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_Express_logo_(2018).svg).
- Discover: [Discover Card mark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Discover_Card_logo.svg).
- Generic card: authored SVG geometry for this project, covered by the project license.
