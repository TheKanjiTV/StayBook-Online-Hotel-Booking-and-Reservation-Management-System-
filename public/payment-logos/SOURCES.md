# Payment marks

These marks identify payment preferences in a booking interface. They do not indicate a connected gateway, provider authorization or a completed charge. Trademarks belong to their respective owners. Keep original artwork and proportions.

- GCash: [GCash wordmark on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GCash_logo.svg).
- Maya: [Maya website](https://www.maya.ph/), `/hubfs/Maya/maya_logo_mgreen.svg`.
- Apple Pay: original SVG from the [Apple Pay mark package and guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/).
- Google Pay: original PNG from [Google Pay brand guidelines](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines).
- Visa: [Visa 2021 mark on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Visa_2021.svg).
- Mastercard: [Mastercard 2019 symbol on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mastercard_2019_logo.svg).
- Generic card and bank-transfer symbols: authored SVG geometry for this project; covered by the project's license.
